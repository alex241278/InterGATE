\section{Analysis registry}
\label{sec:registry}
This retrospective inventory identifies the estimand, the analysis family and the evidence available about timing. The records comprise the versioned local protocol, immutable model and panel manifests, and notebook outputs. A rule fixed before a particular evaluation may have been chosen after earlier results were available. The local locks document that narrower chronology. There was no public prospective registration identified for the complete study. Primary denotes the role of an outcome in the submitted specification. All analyses added for this revision are post hoc with respect to the submission and reuse previously evaluated external cohorts where indicated.
